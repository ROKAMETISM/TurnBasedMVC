2D Flat grid with no cover
Player take turn, then Enemy (or other faction) take turn
2AP, movement 1ap, etc
Player Units : Weapon, Armor and Items
Enemy Units : Alien with abilities.
Before each tactical mission, player composes the team.
Team size : 4 ~ 
Strategy Layer <-> Tactical Layer
Weapon : AR / SG / SR / MG / Melee
AR : 1AP, 0~65%, mid-far to close range
SG : 1AP, 0~100%, close range
SR : 2AP, 80~20%, far range
MG : 1AP, 70%, mid to close range, can shoot twice or move and shoot
Melee : 1AP, 90%, 1 tile range
Reload = 1AP, no turn end
Hunker down = 1AP, defense + 30%
Armor : fast but less defense / More defensive but slow / extra special effect armors
Item : Grenade, Medkit, Flashbang
